Our apps do not require account creation, login, or registration. We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address.

Our apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect and process information including:

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with its own privacy policies. We do not control or access this information.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Firebase Storage

Our apps may use Google Firebase Storage to host and deliver wallpapers, images, audio files, and other content.

When content is accessed, Firebase may process standard technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps, and network diagnostics as part of normal service operation.

We use Firebase Storage only for content delivery and do not use Firebase Authentication, Firestore, or Firebase Analytics unless explicitly stated within a specific app.

4. Permissions Used

Depending on the Android version and app functionality, our apps may request:

  • INTERNET – To load wallpapers, media, and advertisements.
  • SET_WALLPAPER – To apply wallpapers to the device.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES – To access saved wallpapers when required.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – For compatibility with older Android versions.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – For saving wallpapers on older Android versions.

We do not access your contacts, messages, microphone, camera, or precise location unless explicitly stated by a specific app feature.

5. Local Storage

Some information such as favorites, settings, cached wallpapers, and user preferences may be stored locally on your device.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.
